This polychoron is obtained by rectifying teh uniform Snub 24-cell. I created this model upon discovering from the Hi.Gher.Space wiki that it was CRF, and since the Snub 24-cell lends itself to easy Zome construction due to being a diminishing of the 600-cell. This polytope is not isogonal, and the 2 distinct vertex orbits are colored in white and green (these correspond to the edge orbits of the Snub 24-cell).
